## Slide 5: The Mathematical Matching Engine
* **Formulaic Transparency:**
  $$\text{Fit Score} = 0.40 \times \text{Core Match} + 0.20 \times \text{Supp Match} + 0.20 \times \text{Affinity} + 0.20 \times \text{TF-IDF Cosine Similarity}$$
* **Scikit-Learn NLP:** Converts student coursework and career track profiles into TF-IDF vector embeddings to identify semantic alignment.
* **Honest Ethical Stance:** Fit scores measure educational profile-to-role alignment, not a guaranteed hiring probability.
